About

I'm a Master of IT student in Sydney specialising in security, working toward a career in defensive security. Most candidates for that work have never exploited anything — I have, and it changes how you read a log.

My background is hands-on web exploitation (stored and reflected XSS, SQL injection, filter bypass, attribute injection) and applied cryptography. I turn that offensive knowledge into detection: when a SIEM surfaces an injection attempt, I already know what the attacker was reaching for and what comes next.

Everything here is documented the way a professional finding is — reproduction, impact, remediation — not just a flag and a screenshot.
