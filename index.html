shors-rsa-cracker.py
RSA implemented from scratch, classical factoring attacks, a from-scratch quantum statevector simulator cross-validated against Google Cirq, and a real run submitted to actual IBM quantum hardware — an end-to-end demonstration of why (and when) quantum computing threatens RSA.

threshold_signatures.py
An implementation of Kelsey, Lang & Lucks (2024) — Winternitz one-time signatures, Merkle authentication trees, and a k-of-n threshold signing protocol — with every primitive computed live in the browser via a real multi-tab demo over BroadcastChannel, not simulated in one shared array.
PythonJavaScriptMerkle Trees
live repo
haven.js
A local-first task app where every task is encrypted client-side (AES-256-GCM, key derived via PBKDF2-SHA256 at 600,000 iterations) before it ever touches storage or the network. No account required, works offline, and a live "you vs. the server" reveal proves the encryption claim instead of just stating it.
